Instructions for playing:

At the start of the game you will be presented with 5 cards. You can choose to
discard any or all of the 5 cards by entering 0-4 and pressing enter for each
card you want to discard. Once you're done discarding, you need to enter D and
press the return key. Then you will be presented with your final hand and what
your best combination is.
